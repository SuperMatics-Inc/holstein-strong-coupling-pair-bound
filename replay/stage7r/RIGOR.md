**Step 1 (Decomposition of $\mathcal{H}_e$ by total bond particle number $N_e = n_u + n_v \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}$).** Both $B_e$ and $N_{\mathrm{odd},e}$ conserve the total bond electron number $N_e = n_u + n_v$ (and in fact conserve $(N_{e,\uparrow}, N_{e,\downarrow})$ separately), so $\mathcal{H}_e = \bigoplus_{N_e=0}^4 \mathcal{H}_{e,N_e}$ reduces $R_e(g,\alpha)$ block-diagonally:

  $$R_e(g,\alpha)\big|_{N_e \in \{0,4\}} = \frac{2\,g^2}{\alpha}\,I_2 \succeq 0.$$

  $$R_e(g,\alpha)\big|_{N_e \in \{1,3\}} = \alpha\,I_4 + \frac{2\,g^2}{\alpha}\,I_4 - g\,B_e = \left(\sqrt{\alpha}\,I_4 - \frac{g}{2\sqrt{\alpha}}\,B_e\right)^{\!2} + \frac{7\,g^2}{4\,\alpha}\,I_4 \succeq 0. \tag{3.8}$$

    $$|p_\uparrow\rangle := |1,0,1,0\rangle \;\;(n_{u,\uparrow}=n_{v,\uparrow}=1), \qquad |p_\downarrow\rangle := |0,1,0,1\rangle \;\;(n_{u,\downarrow}=n_{v,\downarrow}=1).$$

### 4.1 Exact CAR sign gauge on a single bond: zero fermionic sign cancellation (PROVED)

Consider the smallest possible connected polymer in `STAGE6/RIGOR.md` §3: a single undirected nearest-neighbor bond $E = \{e\}$ with $e = \{u,v\}$ and $V(E) = \{u,v\}$. In the physical grand-canonical trace on $\{u,v\}$, the undirected physical hopping operator on $e$ is
$$T_e := \sum_{\sigma \in \{\uparrow, \downarrow\}} \bigl(c_{u,\sigma}^\dagger c_{v,\sigma} + c_{v,\sigma}^\dagger c_{u,\sigma}\bigr), \tag{4.1}$$
acting on the $16$-dimensional two-site Fock space $\mathcal{H}_e = \mathrm{span}\bigl\{|n_{u,\uparrow}, n_{u,\downarrow}, n_{v,\uparrow}, n_{v,\downarrow}\rangle\bigr\}$, ordered as modes $(1, 2, 3, 4) = \bigl((u,\uparrow), (u,\downarrow), (v,\uparrow), (v,\downarrow)\bigr)$.

**Lemma 4.1 (Single-Bond Jordan-Wigner Gauge Equivalence).** *Define the diagonal unitary operator $U_{\mathrm{JW}} \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 16}$ on $\mathcal{H}_e$ by*
$$U_{\mathrm{JW}}\,|n_{u,\uparrow}, n_{u,\downarrow}, n_{v,\uparrow}, n_{v,\downarrow}\rangle := (-1)^{n_{u,\downarrow}\,n_{v,\uparrow}}\,|n_{u,\uparrow}, n_{u,\downarrow}, n_{v,\uparrow}, n_{v,\downarrow}\rangle. \tag{4.2}$$
*Then $U_{\mathrm{JW}} = U_{\mathrm{JW}}^\dagger = U_{\mathrm{JW}}^{-1}$ commutes with every site-occupation operator $n_{u,\sigma}, n_{v,\sigma}$ (and hence with the atomic Hamiltonian $D_0$ and all phonon source densities $n_u, n_v$), and conjugates the physical CAR hopping operator $T_e$ (4.1) into the entrywise nonnegative signless operator $B_e = B_e^{(1)}$ (3.3):*
$$U_{\mathrm{JW}}\,T_e\,U_{\mathrm{JW}}^{-1} = B_e^{(1)}. \tag{4.3}$$
*Consequently, for every integer $m \ge 1$ and every closed sequence of basis states $\eta_0 \to \eta_1 \to \dots \to \eta_m = \eta_0$ in $\mathcal{H}_e$,*
$$\prod_{j=1}^m \langle \eta_j | T_e | \eta_{j-1} \rangle = \prod_{j=1}^m \langle \eta_j | B_e^{(1)} | \eta_{j-1} \rangle \in \{0, +1\}. \tag{4.4}$$

*Proof.* Under the mode ordering $(1,2,3,4) = \bigl((u,\uparrow), (u,\downarrow), (v,\uparrow), (v,\downarrow)\bigr)$, hopping spin $\uparrow$ between modes $1$ and $3$ passes over mode $2 = (u,\downarrow)$, so in the occupation basis $\langle \eta' | (c_{u,\uparrow}^\dagger c_{v,\uparrow} + c_{v,\uparrow}^\dagger c_{u,\uparrow}) | \eta \rangle = (-1)^{n_{u,\downarrow}} \langle \eta' | B_{e,\uparrow} | \eta \rangle$ while $n_{v,\uparrow}$ flips between $0$ and $1$. Likewise, hopping spin $\downarrow$ between modes $2$ and $4$ passes over mode $3 = (v,\uparrow)$, so $\langle \eta' | (c_{u,\downarrow}^\dagger c_{v,\downarrow} + c_{v,\downarrow}^\dagger c_{u,\downarrow}) | \eta \rangle = (-1)^{n_{v,\uparrow}} \langle \eta' | B_{e,\downarrow} | \eta \rangle$ while $n_{u,\downarrow}$ flips between $0$ and $1$.
In both cases, the product $n_{u,\downarrow} n_{v,\uparrow}$ changes by $n_{u,\downarrow}$ (for a spin-$\uparrow$ hop) or by $n_{v,\uparrow}$ (for a spin-$\downarrow$ hop), so the diagonal gauge factor $(-1)^{n_{u,\downarrow} n_{v,\uparrow}}$ changes by $(-1)^{n_{u,\downarrow}}$ or $(-1)^{n_{v,\uparrow}}$ respectively, cancelling the CAR Jordan-Wigner sign on every matrix element! This proves (4.3). Taking diagonal matrix elements of products along any closed trajectory $\eta_0 \to \dots \to \eta_m = \eta_0$ cancels the boundary gauge factors $U_{\mathrm{JW}}(\eta_0)^2 = (+1)^2 = 1$, giving (4.4). $\square$

- **Sector $\mathcal{S}_\downarrow^{(v)}$ (spin $\uparrow$ frozen at site $v$, starting at $|0_v\rangle$):** Fix $n_{u,\uparrow}(s) \equiv 0$ and $n_{v,\uparrow}(s) \equiv 1$ for all $s \in [0,\beta]$ (using only $T_{e,\downarrow}$ hops), and require $\eta_0 = \eta_{2r} = |0_v\rangle = |0,0,1,1\rangle$ (where $(n_u,n_v) = (0,2)$ and $E_{\mathrm{at}}(|0_v\rangle) = 0$). For every $r \ge 1$, these **four** families of $2r$-hop closed trajectories are pairwise disjoint (since the ordered pair $(\eta_0, \eta_1)$ uniquely identifies the family) and have identical weights.

The atomic energy along $\eta(\cdot)$ is $\int_0^\beta E_{\mathrm{at}}(\eta(s))\,ds = U\,|A|$, and by Theorem 4.2 (4.11)–(4.12), the exact chronological phonon action on this two-state trajectory satisfies $\Phi_\beta[\eta] \le U\,|A|$ for **every** $\Omega > 0$. $$e^{-\int_0^\beta E_{\mathrm{at}}(\eta(s))\,ds - \Phi_\beta[\eta]} = e^{-U|A| - \Phi_\beta[\eta]} \ge e^{-2\,U\,|A|} \ge e^{-3\,U\,|A|} \qquad (\forall\,\Omega > 0). \tag{4.21}$$

### 4.4 Physical interpretation of the Route 3 and Route 4 results

Theorem 4.2 and Theorem 4.3 clarify the exact mechanism that governs the unresummed support-polymer expansion in the slow-phonon regime $\Omega \ll t^2/U$:
1. **High- versus low-frequency behavior of $\Phi_\beta(\Delta)$ and why slow phonons do not diverge on brief excursions:** For a fixed two-state excursion of duration $\Delta \in (0,\beta)$ at fixed $\beta$, taking $\Omega \to \infty$ gives $\Phi_\beta(\Delta) \sim U/\Omega \to 0$ (fast phonons adjust instantaneously save for jump corners), whereas taking $\Omega \to 0^+$ increases $\Phi_\beta(\Delta)$ to its slow-phonon limit $U\Delta(1 - \Delta/\beta) > 0$. Crucially, however, because $\int_0^\beta K_{\beta,\Omega}(s-u)\,du = U/2$ is independent of $\Omega$, the slow-phonon increase never exceeds the universal cap $\Phi_\beta[n] = U|A| - 2\iint_{A\times A} K_{\beta,\Omega}(s-u)\,ds\,du \le U|A|$ (Theorem 4.2 (4.10)–(4.11)). Thus over a brief virtual excursion of duration $\Delta \sim 1/U \ll 1/\Omega$, the phonon action adds at most $U\Delta = O(1)$ to the bare atomic cost $U\Delta$, shifting the effective odd-odd energy gap from $U$ to at most $2U$ rather than suppressing the hop by an $(\Omega \to 0^+)$-divergent penalty.
2. **Why each excursion is brief ($\Delta \sim 1/(2U)$) while the number of excursions grows with $\beta$:** In the damped comparison model $H_{2,2} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -t \\ -t & 2U \end{pmatrix}$ of Theorem 4.3, each individual $2$-hop excursion is cut off at duration $\Delta \sim 1/(2U)$ by the combined atomic-plus-phonon odd-odd damping $e^{-2U\Delta}$ regardless of how small $\Omega > 0$ is. Across all orders $r \ge 1$, $H_{2,2}$ has exact eigenvalues $\lambda_{\mp,2} = U \mp \sqrt{U^2 + t^2}$ (so $|\lambda_{-,2}| = \sqrt{U^2 + t^2} - U = \frac{t^2}{U + \sqrt{U^2+t^2}}$) and ground-state projection weight $c_{-,2} = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(1 + \frac{U}{\sqrt{U^2+t^2}}\bigr) \in (\frac{1}{2}, 1)$, giving the exact all-order growth $\langle 0 | e^{-\beta H_{2,2}} | 0 \rangle = c_{-,2}\,e^{\beta(\sqrt{U^2+t^2}-U)} + (1 - c_{-,2})\,e^{-\beta(\sqrt{U^2+t^2}+U)} \sim c_{-,2}\exp\!\bigl(\beta(\sqrt{U^2+t^2}-U)\bigr)$ as $\beta \to \infty$ at fixed $(t,U) \in (0,\infty)^2$, uniformly in $\Omega > 0$. In the small-$t/U$ limit $t/U \to 0$, the exact rate expands as $\sqrt{U^2+t^2}-U = \frac{t^2}{2U} - \frac{t^4}{8U^3} + O(t^6/U^5)$ with $c_{-,2} \to 1$, recovering the leading second-order superexchange rate $\beta t^2/(2U)$.
3. **Scope of the method barrier:** Because a single isolated bond $e = \{e\}$ has zero fermionic sign cancellation (Lemma 4.1) and unresummed polymer activity $w(\{e\}) > 1$ for $\tau \le \min(U,\,t^2/(36U),\,t/4)$ uniformly in $\Omega > 0$ (Theorem 4.3), the ordinary unresummed atomic logarithmic polymer series (which contains $\log(1 + w(\{e\})) = \sum_{n \ge 1} (-1)^{n-1} w(\{e\})^n / n$) cannot converge absolutely below $\tau \asymp \min(t^2/U, t)$. This is a rigorous barrier for the unresummed atomic support-polymer expansion, while leaving dressed/resummed reference expansions **OPEN**.
