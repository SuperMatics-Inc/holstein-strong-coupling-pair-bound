## 3. Remaining Gaps (`OPEN` / `[INFERENCE]`)

1. **Moderate/weak coupling ($t/U \gtrsim 1$) and temperatures below $2^{27} t^2/U$ at slow phonon frequency ($\Omega \ll t^2/U$) (`OPEN`):**
   While Theorem 3.3 removes the $t/U \le 2^{-21}$ restriction completely and lowers the strong-coupling prefactor by $2048\times$ to $C_7 = 2^{27}$, its temperature scale $2^{27} t^2/U$ still grows quadratically in $t$ and sits above `STAGE4`'s bare-hopping bound $1024 t$ once $t/U \ge 2^{-17}$. In the deep adiabatic regime $y = \Omega/U < y_{\mathrm{cross}}^{(4,7)}(x)$ (or $x \ge 2^{-17}$ and $y < y_{\mathrm{cross}}^{(4,\mathrm{hop})}(x)$), `LINEAR4`'s $\Omega^{2/3}$ ceiling remains the best proved bound.
2. **Dressed or resummed reference expansions beyond the unresummed atomic polymer expansion (`OPEN` / `[INFERENCE]`):**
   Theorem 4.3 proves that the unresummed atomic logarithmic polymer expansion (`STAGE6`/`STAGE7R`) encounters $w(\{e\}) > 1$ for $\tau \le \min(U,\,t^2/(36U),\,t/4)$ uniformly in $\Omega > 0$. **[INFERENCE]** Any cluster expansion that penetrates $\tau \ll t^2/U$ or achieves a linear-in-$\Omega$ ceiling at fixed $(t,U)$ must resum single-bond neutral returns (such as $\log(1 + w(\{e\}))$ or a dressed dimer/polaronic reference propagator) rather than expanding in raw atomic support polymers around $D_0$. Whether such a resummed expansion can be controlled across overlapping bonds remains **OPEN**.
